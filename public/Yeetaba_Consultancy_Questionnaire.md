# Ye'Etaba Consultancy: Website Questionnaire

## 1. About Ye'Etaba
**Company Profile & Mission:**
Ye'Etaba Consultancy is a young-women-founded and led firm built on lived experience, technical expertise, and a deep commitment to meaningful, lasting impact. Founded by two sisters, the firm brings together strategy, research, communication, safeguarding, ESG, and implementation to support organizations across development, business, and public sectors.

**Mission:**
To help those who do meaningful work better by strengthening how they think, operate, and engage with those they serve.

**Origins & Problem Solved:**
The firm is rooted in the inheritance of care and leadership from the founders' grandmother and mother. It was founded to address a clear gap: many organizations doing important work lack the systems, strategy, and ethical frameworks (safeguarding, gender inclusion, ESG) needed to sustain, communicate, and scale their impact. Ye'Etaba bridges this gap with practical, responsible solutions grounded in real context.
