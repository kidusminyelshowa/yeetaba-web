# Our Experience

At **Ye’Etaba Consultancy**, we create work that connects[cite: 3]. 

Through storytelling, strategy, advocacy, facilitation, and creative execution, we partner with organizations and movements committed to social impact, gender justice, youth empowerment, safeguarding, and community transformation[cite: 3]. Our work is rooted in the belief that meaningful engagement should feel human, intentional, and lasting[cite: 3].

---

## Empathy for Life Integrated Development Association (ELiDA)

Ye’Etaba has led and supported a range of advocacy, branding, storytelling, and public engagement initiatives for ELiDA, helping strengthen both the organization’s visibility and its connection with communities and audiences[cite: 3].

* **Rebranding Journey:** Supported ELiDA’s rebranding through refreshed communication approaches, visual storytelling direction, audience engagement strategies, and brand-centered public experiences that strengthened the organization’s public identity and voice[cite: 3].
* **CSOs Week Exhibition:** Led the conceptualization, creative direction, and facilitation of ELiDA’s exhibition presence, transforming their booth into an immersive storytelling and engagement space[cite: 3]. This included interactive experiences, visual storytelling, documentary screenings, branded advocacy materials, and audience-centered activities centered on gender justice, peacebuilding, and violence prevention[cite: 3].
* **National Panel Discussion:** Developed and facilitated a national panel discussion during CSOs Week on **Technology-Facilitated Gender-Based Violence (TFGBV)**, bringing together diverse voices to foster dialogue on online violence, digital safety, gender justice, and the realities women and girls face in digital spaces[cite: 3].
* **16 Days of Activism:** Conceptualized and facilitated the organization’s 2025 16 Days of Activism closing event[cite: 3]. Through creative event direction, audience engagement, branded advocacy materials, and overall coordination, Ye'Etaba designed an emotionally grounded and reflective space that encouraged conversation, awareness, solidarity, and collective reflection around gender-based violence[cite: 3].
* **Documentary Impact Stories:** Led the field coordination, interviews, scripting, filming, and post-production of documentary impact stories across project sites in the Wollo area of the Amhara Region[cite: 3]. These pieces documented stories of women’s leadership, girls’ education, peacebuilding, and community resilience, translating lived experiences into emotionally grounded visual narratives[cite: 3].

## Women Deliver

Ye’Etaba developed the **Telela App**, a youth-centered, holistic wellbeing platform created to support adolescents and young people through accessible, engaging, and human-centered digital learning experiences[cite: 3].

* **Holistic Topics:** Explored areas including Sexual and Reproductive Health and Rights (SRHR), mental health, nutrition, puberty education, healthy relationships, life skills, and overall wellbeing in an inclusive, relatable manner[cite: 3].
* **Platform Design:** Led the conceptualization, content structuring, communication flow, and creative engagement approach[cite: 3]. This shaped Telela into a safe, interactive digital space where young people could learn, reflect, and access trusted information to encourage confidence, self-awareness, and healthy personal development[cite: 3].
